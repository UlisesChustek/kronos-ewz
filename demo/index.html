How to read it

Each thin line is one sampled future. The band is the 5–95th percentile of 64 paths; the solid line is the median. The P(up) confidence interval measures Monte Carlo sampling uncertainty within the model, not its accuracy or calibration against the market.

What it is not

Not a trading signal. On the held-out test set the fine-tuned model does not beat a random walk on point error, and its 45.1% directional accuracy equals always predicting down. Why it failed →

Session data

EWZ trades in US hours, so 24 hourly bars are roughly 5 calendar days — not 24 hours. The 7-bar model is the ~1 trading-day view.

Method

Reproducible, precomputed, and honest

Pipeline

  1. Dukascopy EWZ.US-USD 1h BID bars → UTC session data.
  2. 512-bar lookback, normalized on the lookback only.
  3. Fine-tuned tokenizer + predictor; 64 Monte Carlo paths.
  4. 5–95% band, median path, and terminal-return metrics.

Evaluation

  1. Chronological 80/10/10 split; test is 2025-11 → 2026-10.
  2. Calibration fit on validation only; test inspected repeatedly during development.
  3. Bootstrap CIs for MAE/RMSE deltas, Wilson CIs for direction.
  4. Compared with zero-shot and a random-walk baseline.

Caveats

  • CFD feed volume, not consolidated exchange volume.
  • EWZ only; no cross-sectional or macro information.
  • Kronos upstream is MIT-licensed; no separate project license is included. Not investment advice.
  • Probabilities are raw model frequencies, not calibrated.
